# Code of Conduct

LabNet follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive
criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community.
Harassment, insults, and publishing others' private information are not
acceptable, in the project's spaces or when representing it elsewhere.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues
and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may warn, suspend
or ban a contributor, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
